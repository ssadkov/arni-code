# Delivery formats

Choose only formats the user requested. These are editable generic defaults, not platform limits or a particular author's permanent rules.

- Editorial plan: audience, central claim, hook, numbered sections, needed source/visual evidence, proposed ending and unresolved factual questions.
- Long video: title alternatives, description, chapters based on the final video, supplied links, thumbnail brief. Do not invent timestamps from an outline.
- Short clip: source start/end, complete opening/context/payoff, suggested on-screen text, caption and optional call to action. Preserve caveats and do not assume portrait framing without a requested format.
- Multi-platform package: separate labeled drafts, with a platform-appropriate hook and depth. Research current length/media/link restrictions when necessary rather than hardcoding remembered limits.
- Editorial review: factual blockers, unsupported product claims, meaning changes, voice/clarity issues, proposed corrections and a clean version when requested.
- Author profile proposal: name/terminology, voice, style, links supplied by the user, preferred formats and accepted editing choices. Keep private API credentials outside exported profiles. Show proposed persistent rules before saving them.

When speech and a supplied script disagree materially, identify the timecoded original and proposed wording. A subtitle correction must not imply that the recorded audio changed. Mark unverified names, numbers and dates, and keep a source list for substantive research.
